Philosophy

The drydock standard.

Every visit should make the system easier to trust. The boyscout rule applies to code, clusters, scripts, runbooks, dashboards, and the small operational rituals people forget to name.

01

Leave it cleaner.

Technical debt is rust. Do not paint over it. Remove a little, document the repair, and make the next pass easier.

02

Automate pragmatically.

Automate the recurring, risky, and boring. Leave room for judgment. Not every rope needs a platform team.

03

Compartmentalize.

A breach in one section should not sink the fleet. Boundaries, identity, and clear ownership are operational kindness.
