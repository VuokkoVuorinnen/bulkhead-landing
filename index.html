The Drift

If the architecture feels haunted, the deployment smells improvised, or the AI has been allowed on deck unsupervised, Bulkhead probably recognizes the weather pattern.

01 / Drydock

Refit First.

Decorative transformation is not the point. Rust is rust. Strip it, plate it, document the repair, then discuss velocity.

02 / Compartment

Flood One Section, Not The Fleet.

Namespaces, trust boundaries, identity, clean interfaces, escape hatches. A vessel without internal walls is just optimism with a website.

03 / Machine Crew

AI Rows. Humans Navigate.

Prompt the boilerplate. Generate the drudgery. Audit the output like your insurance depends on it.

04 / Weather

Idempotency In A Choppy Sea.

Midnight shell sessions below the waterline are not a process. Repair in code. Repeat cleanly. Sleep.
